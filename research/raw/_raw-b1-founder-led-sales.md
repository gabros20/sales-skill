## 1. The existence gate — when does an assisted sale exist at all?

**@patio11** · 2017-12-01 · https://x.com/patio11/status/936617094166089728 · OPINION
> "The hardest problem in B2C is distribution. The hardest problem in B2B is sales. AppAmaGooBookSoft
> are AppAmaGooBookSoft primarily because they have mortal locks on distribution."

The cleanest one-line statement of why a sales pack exists for B2B and why B2C routes to marketing —
useful as the pack's opening frame. (This is the *category* claim, not the price-point threshold;
d4 holds the Janz-vs-patio11 threshold disagreement.)

**@Shpigford** · 2026-06-16 · https://x.com/Shpigford/status/2066881769027047658 · **OPINION — existence gate, inverted**
> "if you still require an enterprise sales call to give pricing, you're going to be eaten alive in
> the long run. in most cases, clever engineers just need a day or two w/ AI to build the exact part
> of your product they need. they can literally build it before you can hop on call"

Why it matters: an argument that the assisted motion is being **eroded from below** by
build-it-yourself economics — the gate is moving, not fixed. Directly contests the "gate pricing
behind an enterprise call" default.

**@arvidkahl** · 2025-06-14 · https://x.com/arvidkahl/status/1933686087462830355 · OPINION
> "I have the nagging feeling that low-price commodity SaaS (social chedulers, calendar link tools,
> job site generators) will be almost completely absorbed by people's vibecoded solutions. The last
> bastion of SaaS will be the 'highly integrated b2b used-by-several-teams and full o…"

✅ **RE-FETCHED IN FULL 2026-08-03** — the truncated half is the valuable half:

> "The relationship-building of sales, the custom solution work required to land and retain a larger
> business: all of that is much less tech than it depends on interpersonal dynamics. **And as long as
> humans make tech choices instead of AI systems**, these companies will possess a moat that prevents
> them from being cloned by a well-written prompt."

The same economics from the seller's side: what survives is exactly the multi-team, integrated,
complex product — the class that *requires* an assisted sale. Two independent practitioners
converging on "complexity is what's left" is a real signal for the existence gate.

**What makes the full version much stronger: it states its own falsification condition.** The moat
holds *as long as humans make the tech choices*. That gives the pack a testable boundary for the
existence gate rather than an assertion — and it names the exact condition (AI systems making
purchase decisions) under which a sales pack's assumptions would need revisiting.

**@tylertringas** · 2020-06-16 · https://x.com/tylertringas/status/1272897060799479814 · OPINION
> "Modern software founder MBA curriculum: * Enough code to be dangerous + no-code prototyping
> * Writing, Note system, GTD, project mgmt * Founder-led Sales, Content/Email marketing
> * Positioning, Pricing, Strategy * Inbound recruiting (eg Twitter) * Remote HR + Operations"

Founder-led sales listed as a core competence distinct from marketing — supports the pack's seam.

## 2. Founder-led selling in practice — first-person

**@arvidkahl** · 2026-03-02 · https://x.com/arvidkahl/status/1896219399369249126 · **FIRST-PERSON — the central tension**
> "Founder-led sales is both fun and super scary. Not only do you have to deliver a product worth
> buying, which requires a realistic/skeptical mindset, but you have to convince someone to be
> excited about it, for which you need optimism and trust. I find that very hard to balance!"

The best available statement of the *psychological* problem the solo reader actually faces: the
builder's skepticism and the seller's optimism are opposing stances held by one person. Strongest
founder-led exhibit here, and it is a first-person admission of difficulty, not advice.

**@arvidkahl** · 2026-03-04 · https://x.com/arvidkahl/status/1897011837893550461 · **FIRST-PERSON — honesty-line adjacent**

> on the glasses and the moustache :D"

A solo founder deciding whether to present as a team. Light in tone, but it is exactly the disclosure
question the pack must answer for one-person companies — and it rhymes with the AI-SDR disclosure
question in b2.

**@patio11** · 2020-12-17 · https://x.com/patio11/status/1339412793687040001 · OPINION

## 3. Pricing presentation and discounting discipline

(Pricing/tier DESIGN is product's per the charter; below is presentation, negotiation, and the
founder's own pricing decisions.)

**@dvassallo** · 2022-12-17 · https://x.com/dvassallo/status/1604230833476620288 · OPINION
> "Pricing is 99% psychology, 1% math."

**@dvassallo** · 2022-11-04 · https://x.com/dvassallo/status/1588344841112793088 · OPINION
> "The reason people pay $8/day for a Starbucks but balk at $8/mo for Twitter verification is because
> pricing is almost all about psychology. If you think pricing is rational, you'll be at a huge
> disadvantage when setting your own prices."

**@dvassallo** · 2023-04-01 · https://x.com/dvassallo/status/1641958262403899392 · **FIRST-PERSON RESULT — best pricing number in this file**
> "Just hit $100K revenue on Gumroad as Q1 comes to an end. If I move to Stripe I could save about
> $10K in fees next quarter. But here's why I'm staying on Gumroad: 1. PPP — $18,340 of my sales came
> from turning on PPP (purchasing power parity) pricing on Feb 1. Yes, maybe some of…"

✅ **RE-FETCHED IN FULL 2026-08-03.** The complete hedge:

> "$18,340 of my sales came from turning on PPP (purchasing power parity) pricing on Feb 1. **Yes,
> maybe some of these sales would have happened anyway at full price, but I'm sure this helped
> increase revenue.**"

Every bucket in the post carries its own counterfactual in the same form ("Yes, most customers would
have likely chosen to use a credit card…", "Yes, over time I would likely be able to rank well too…").
**This is a model of honest attribution by a solo founder** — the claim and its limit travel together.

⚠️ **BONUS FINDING — the buckets provably cannot be summed.** Full breakdown of ~$100K Q1 revenue:
PPP $18,340 · PayPal $18,850 · affiliates $5,370 · Gumroad Discover $999 · Google $14,727 · existing
Gumroad accounts $71,889 (71%+). **These total $130,175 — about 30% MORE than the $100K they
decompose** — because the categories overlap (a channel, a payment method, and an account status can
describe the same sale). The author never claims they add up; a careless reader would.
**Recommend this as a teaching exhibit in its own right:** attribution buckets that overlap by
construction, with arithmetic the reader can check in one line. It echoes marketing's attribution
flagship from the founder's side.

**@dvassallo** · 2022-05-30 · https://x.com/dvassallo/status/1531338262333165569 · **FOLKLORE — never-ship candidate**
> "The science says that a $10 product sells significantly more when priced $9.99. Yet, I prefer
> pricing my products in multiples of $5. A $9.99 price makes me feel as if the seller is taking
> advantage of a bug in my brain. I prefer if my customers don't experience that feeling."

"The science says" with no citation, asserting an undefined magnitude. Classic charm-pricing folklore.
Note the author declines to follow it — so the tweet is both a folklore specimen and a reasoned
refusal, which makes it a strong teaching pair.

**@tylertringas** · 2020-04-18 · https://x.com/tylertringas/status/1251527967068602368 · OPINION

## 4. Win/loss learning — the strongest single find in this file

**@patio11** · 2021-01-04 · https://x.com/patio11/status/1345906043197427713 · **OPINION — flagship-adjacent, highest value in b1**
> "A good discipline to get into with regards to any form of sales or account management: *Write
> down* 'If we lose this deal, it will be because of X.' in advance. If you lose the deal/account/etc,
> ask why you lost it. Check against your prediction, and get better at predicting."

Why this is the most important capture in b1: it is a **written-prediction-then-scored-outcome loop**
— exactly what the rest of the corpus is missing. b3 established a three-surface NULL (nobody
backtests stage weights; no practitioner reports a measured forecast accuracy). This supplies the
*mechanism* that would fix it, at solo scale, with no CRM and no statistics: predict the loss reason
in advance, check it, calibrate. It serves win/loss learning (a charter "Includes" item) and the
forecasting-honesty flagship simultaneously. **Recommended as a candidate flagship primitive**,
credited to patio11.

**@patio11** · 2020-01-27 · https://x.com/patio11/status/1221683564950867968 · OPINION
> "…being organized, treating it as a sales pipeline, pursuing multiple offers in parallel, and
> practicing for interviews."

Pipeline thinking applied to a job search — a useful analogy for readers with no sales background.
